# Ten local models on four instruments

Measured 2026-09-18 at `cb49ccf`. The last model comparison
(`DECISIONS.md`, 2026-09-14, "nine local models on one corpus") ran
against eighteen eval cases, a single planning call and no narrowing.
Everything about that setup has since changed - two-stage planning,
`v6-unmatched-filter` wording, narrowing at K=20, a 34-case eval suite, a
mid instrument and a multi-turn instrument - so the default model's own
justification had expired.

Only the model varies here. Two stages, wording `v6-unmatched-filter`,
thinking off, temperature 0, narrowing on at K=20 with `e5-large-q8` +
`bge-reranker-v2-m3-q8`, `ORCHESTRA_PLANNER_TODAY=2026-09-16`, every Jev
flag unset.

Models over ~13 GB (`qwen38-27b-iq3s`, `qwen36-35b-iq4xs`,
`gemma4-26b-a4b-qat`, `gpt-oss-20b`) were excluded by decision: they
cannot share the 16 GB card with the embedding and reranking models the
corpus and mid instruments need, and 2026-09-14 already recorded that
size does not help here.

**Method note, learned the hard way:** llama-swap must be emptied
(`GET /unload`) before each model, or the previous model still holds the
card, the new one fails to start, and every case reads 0 - one such run
was discarded before the numbers below were taken.

## What each model does

**`qwen3.5-9b-q8` (the default).** The only model at 34/34 on the eval
suite. Middling elsewhere: corpus 77, mid refusals 16/20. Its one
dialogue loss is `d11` (詳細を見せて with no id named), a defect of the
product, not of the model - `TODO.md` carries it.

**`gemma4-12b-q8`.** Best corpus (79), best dialogues (**27/27** - it
answers the `d11` turn the default gets wrong), zero false refusals on
mid. Its two eval losses are both "fills in more than it was asked":
`real-register-screws` answers a form 10/10 where a call is expected, and
`real-tanaka-attendance` asks about `kind` 10/10. Costs ~50% more time
per question than the default (2022 ms mean on the corpus).

**`gemma4-12b` (q4 QAT).** The same family a quantisation down: mid
answerable **39/40**, the best of any model, at 1690 ms against q8's 2022. Loses on the corpus (73 against 79) and two dialogue turns
(`att-002は？` after an inventory turn → none; 在庫を登録したい →
list_capabilities).

**`qwen3.5-9b` (Q4_K_M).** The default's own weights, lighter: eval
31/34, corpus identical at 77, mid refusals **better** (18/20 against
16/20), dialogues equal at 26/27, and **14% faster** (1198 ms against
1363). Its three eval losses are the built-in-choice rows
(`real-what-day` → list_capabilities, `real-capability-inventory` →
none) plus `real-register-new-item`. A quantisation step costs very
little on this task.

**`ornith15-9b-q8`.** Strong refusal (18/20) and eval 32/34, but corpus
60/100 - it does not find the right operation in a thousand. Both eval
losses answer `none` to a create request; four of its five dialogue
losses are create-or-follow-up turns.

**`granite41-8b-q8` - the instrument trap.** Corpus 78/100, a point above
the default. eval 15/34 and dialogues 7/27. On the real six-operation
catalogue it answers `none` 10 times out of 10 on nineteen cases, and
fails twenty dialogue turns in 110-200 ms - it is refusing instantly
rather than working. A comparison run only on the corpus would have
called it a candidate.

**`lfm25-8b-a1b-q8` - the other trap.** The only model to refuse every
impossible question (**20/20**, better than any other model including the
default) - and it does so by refusing nearly everything: 39 of 40
answerable mid questions falsely refused, corpus **1/100**, dialogues
1/27. Judged on refusal alone it wins the table.

**`minicpm5-2b-q8`.** The speed extreme: 475 ms mean on the corpus and
206 ms on the dialogues, a third to a fifth of the default's. Correctness
is well below (eval 24/34, corpus 56), and it drops cross-service
follow-ups (`勤怠の方も見せて`, `att-002は？`) in tens of milliseconds.

**`spark-x25-4b-q8`, `qwen35-4b-q8`.** Both under the default everywhere;
spark loses the first turn of most dialogues outright (138-450 ms), which
is the granite failure shape at a smaller scale.

## What this says about choosing a model

- **No instrument alone ranks these models.** The corpus alone promotes
  `granite41-8b-q8` (78) over the default (77) while that model answers
  almost nothing on the real catalogue. The refusal column alone promotes
  `lfm25-8b-a1b-q8`, which answers 1 of 40. The eval suite alone hides
  that two models beat the default on the corpus and on mid.
- **Quantisation is nearly free here.** `qwen3.5-9b` Q4_K_M matches its
  own Q8 on the corpus and dialogues, beats it on mid refusals, loses one
  eval row, and runs 14% faster. `gemma4-12b`'s q4 QAT build beats its own
  q8 on mid answerable and runs faster, while losing corpus points.
- **The 2026-09-14 conclusion still holds in its own terms** - nothing
  here is good because it is bigger - but its specific ranking does not
  transfer: `gemma4-12b-q8`, second-worst on that table's 破損 column,
  is the strongest challenger to the default today, because the wording
  fix (`v6-unmatched-filter`) closed the failure that table was ranking on.
- **The default is no longer unarguable.** `gemma4-12b-q8` wins three
  instruments of four and ties nothing worse than -2 on eval, at ~50%
  more latency; `qwen3.5-9b` Q4_K_M is the same model 14% faster at -3
  eval rows. Whether to move is a product decision, not a measurement
  one - the eval suite is the contract, and only the default passes it
  whole.

## Raw data

Per-model instrument outputs live in the session scratchpad
(`models/out/<model>/{eval,corpus,mid,dialogue}.txt`, plus `vram.txt`),
and each corpus/mid run also wrote its own
`e2e/shortlist/out/*-model-<model>.jsonl` and `.log` (untracked). The
dialogue runs wrote timestamped files under `e2e/dialogue/out/`.
